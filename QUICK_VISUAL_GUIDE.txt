UNDERSTANDING THE NUMBERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Example Strategy:

INPUT:
  Loan: ₹10L @ 8%
  Investment: 40% Gold (8% APR) + 60% Stock (12% APR)
  Period: 10 years
  Inflation: 6%

OUTPUT:
  Nominal Value:  ₹27,27,078  (Account shows)
  Real Value:     ₹15,22,787  (What it buys today)
  Difference:     ₹12,04,292  (Lost to inflation = 44%)

MEANING:
  ✗ "I have ₹27.27L" (Misleading - doesn't account for inflation)
  ✓ "I have ₹15.23L in today's money" (True purchasing power)

IMPACT:
  ✗ Nominal ROI: 127% (Looks amazing!)
  ✓ Real ROI: 6.7% (Actual benefit after inflation)

LESSON:
  Always look at REAL values, not NOMINAL values!
